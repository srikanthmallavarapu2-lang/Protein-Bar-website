FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our ingredients, nutrition, and shipping.

Baking protein bars with high heat denatures delicate plant amino acids and creates a dry, rubbery, chalky texture. By cold-pressing whole ingredients under pressure, we preserve active micronutrients, healthy natural oils, and a decadent fudgy consistency without relying on artificial softeners or palm oil.
We exclusively use non-GMO European golden pea protein isolate paired with whole roasted nuts and seeds (almonds, cashews, peanuts). This combination creates a complete 9-essential-amino-acid profile with exceptional digestibility and zero bloating.
Yes! All five flavours are 100% plant-based, certified vegan by The Vegan Society, and certified gluten-free (tested to <5ppm gluten). We never use dairy, whey, eggs, gelatin, or artificial additives.
Our 3-Pack Builder lets you choose any combination of our 5 flavours to create your personalized trio. Selecting 3 bars saves you $1.49 compared to buying individual bars, and you can repeat your favourite flavour or mix 3 different ones.
Pulse bars stay fresh for 8 months from their press date when stored in a cool, dry place. For an extra-fudgy, dessert-like treat, many of our athletes keep them in the refrigerator!
We stand behind every bar we make. If you try a flavour and don't love it, simply email hello@pulsebars.com within 30 days of delivery. We will issue an immediate refund or send a different flavour of your choice—no hassle, no return shipping needed.
